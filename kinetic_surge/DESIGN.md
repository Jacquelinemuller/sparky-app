## Brand & Style

The design system is tuned specifically for the neurodivergent cognitive flow of an 11-year-old child navigating ADHD. It reframes daily routines, academic assignments, and chores into an energizing, momentum-generating quest interface. Rooted in an arcade-sleek aesthetic, the visual atmosphere communicates high energy, momentum, and optimism without descending into chaotic visual noise or childish tropes.

The design movement combines Tactile Pop with High-Contrast Precision:
- **Kinetic Feedback & Tangible Control**: Interfaces emulate responsive handheld arcade consoles. Interactive elements feel like mechanical, spring-loaded buttons and collectible tokens. Every interaction yields immediate, gratifying tactile feedback.
- **Executive Function Scaffolding**: Eliminates cognitive fatigue through aggressive structural chunking, unambiguous spatial hierarchies, and unmistakable chromatic wayfinding.
- **High-Arousal Warmth with Reading Serenity**: The vibrant orange energy core creates an active, positive arousal state, balanced by warm, ultra-clean surfaces that avoid ocular glare and preserve focus.

## Colors

Color functions as an externalized cognitive guide. The primary hue transition shifts the energetic center of gravity to `#ff6b00`, acting as an invigorating beacon for primary actions, active quest states, and central momentum cues.

### Functional Domains & Color Roles
- **Vibrant Primary (`#ff6b00`)**: The core engine. Applied to primary completion targets, active quest borders, hero CTA buttons, and immediate focus areas.
- **Electric Cyan-Blue Secondary (`#0284c7`)**: The cognitive focus anchor. Dedicated to schoolwork modules, academic timers, educational challenges, and strategic reference panels.
- **Emerald Mint Tertiary (`#059669`) / Success Mint (`#10b981`)**: The dopamine reward trigger. Reserved exclusively for successful completions, claimed rewards, leveled-up states, and positive reinforcement checkmarks.
- **Arcade Amber (`#f59e0b`)**: The non-punitive alert signal. Highlights active streaks, urgent countdown timers, and bonus point multipliers.
- **Creative Violet (`#8b5cf6`)**: Playful self-expression. Denotes personal avatars, audio notes, free-time logs, and creative downtime.

### Surfaces & Neutral Architecture
- **Backdrop Canvas**: A warm-tinted cream substrate (`#fff7ed` to `#fffaf5`) that replaces clinical blue/cool-grays to induce warmth and lessen reading strain.
- **Foreground Tiles**: Pure high-contrast `#ffffff` containers with subtle warm-amber structural borders (`#f2eae1`), establishing distinct card silhouettes that pop off the canvas.
- **Ink & Typography Neutrals**: Deep warm obsidian neutral (`#272523` for titles and `#44403c` for body copy), retaining superior AAA contrast without the harsh, disorienting vibration of 100% black on white.

## Typography

The typographic pairing balances punchy, arcade-grade geometry with research-backed readability:
- **Headlines & Labels (Outfit)**: Crisp, bold, geometric structures that provide instant legibility and an inspiring arcade-sleek presence. Wide letter counters and bold stems guarantee rapid orientation at a glance.
- **Body & Content (Lexend)**: Scientifically engineered to reduce visual crowding and increase reading velocity. Expanded character spacing, distinct terminal boundaries, and unambiguous glyph forms safeguard against line skipping, dyslexic slips, and working memory fatigue.

### Typographic Rules
- **No Fragile Weights**: Weights under Medium (500) are banned across all levels to keep letterforms stable and readable on warm-tinted and colored surfaces.
- **Loose Line Heights for Reading Tracks**: Body copy uses expanded line heights (`lineHeight` 26px to 28px) to provide clear eye tracking paths for users who tend to scan quickly or lose their place.
- **Restricted All-Caps**: Uppercase formatting is reserved strictly for short `label-sm` category chips and streak milestones. Quest titles and instructions must always remain in sentence case for natural parsing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy is built on an **Isolated Chunking Model** using an 8px modular baseline. Content never appears as a dense, endless scroll. Instead, experiences are segmented into modular, bite-sized "stations" or swipeable steps.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Adapting Grids
- **Mobile (up to 640px)**: 4-column fluid layout with 16px (`space-md`) gutters and outer margins. Quests and tasks expand to 4 columns to create giant, comfortable hit zones.
- **Tablet (641px - 1024px)**: 8-column layout with 24px (`space-lg`) gutters. Surfaces divide into a two-zone layout: Active Focus Quest (left 5 columns) and Daily Level Track / Inventory (right 3 columns).
- **Desktop (1025px+)**: 12-column layout with a hard ceiling of 1140px centered horizontally to keep sightlines tight and avoid saccadic distraction across wide displays.

### Touch Ergonomics
- **The 56px Rule (`target-min`)**: Interactive controls maintain a minimum height and hit box of 56px (3.5rem) to ensure frictionless, unfrustrated tapping during high-motor-energy moments.
- **Perceptual Gaps**: Adjacent unrelated tasks must maintain at least `space-lg` (24px) spacing to avoid visual crowding.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tactile physical depth inspired by high-end mechanical keys, handheld game cartridges, and arcade buttons. Blurred cast shadows are replaced with crisp, physical drop rims and energetic chromatic glow spreads.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Floor)**: The warm cream background canvas (`#fff7ed` / `#fffaf5`) with 0 elevation.
- **Level 1 (Resting Tiles & Cards)**: High-contrast white surfaces framed with a 2px stroke (`#f2eae1`) and an extruded physical lip: `box-shadow: 0 4px 0 0 #e8ded2`.
- **Level 2 (Active Quests & Focus Tiles)**: Elevated interactive targets receive an energizing brand-tinted mechanical drop rim: `box-shadow: 0 6px 0 0 #c25200, 0 12px 24px -4px rgba(255, 107, 0, 0.16)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays)**: Floated dialogs with `box-shadow: 0 8px 0 0 #272523, 0 20px 40px rgba(39, 37, 35, 0.20)`.
- **Pressed State (`level-pressed`)**: When tapped, the element shifts down along the Y-axis by 4px (`transform: translateY(4px)`) and collapses its bottom shadow to `0 0 0 0`, simulating a satisfying mechanical click.

### Energetic Aura
Elements that represent active streaks, critical multipliers, or boss-level quests utilize an animated pulse glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 4px rgba(255, 107, 0, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The design system implements a roundedness factor of `3` (Pill-shaped), infusing every surface with friendly, organic fluidity that removes visual severity and eases hyperactive eye movement.

### Curvature Taxonomy
- **Full Pill (`rounded-full` / 9999px)**: Reserved for primary arcade buttons, status indicator capsules, progress bars, and floating action triggers.
- **Macro Containers (`rounded-xl` / 3rem / 48px)**: Applied to parent task cards, quest modals, and celebratory milestone summary cards.
- **Micro Containers (`rounded-lg` / 2rem / 32px)**: Applied to secondary input enclosures, nested item tiles, and segmented control chips.
- **Inner Accents (`rounded` / 1rem / 16px)**: Applied to nested media thumbnails, icons, and embedded checklists to preserve geometric balance.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Pill Action**: Rendered with full roundedness (`rounded-full`), height of 56px, and filled with vibrant orange (`#ff6b00`). Text is set in Outfit `label-lg` in pure white with a 4px solid drop edge in burnt amber (`#c25200`). When tapped, it translates down 4px with zero shadow.
- **Secondary Arcade Pill**: Pure white pill with a 2px outline in `#f2eae1` and a neutral 4px bottom rim in `#ded2c3`. Font color is `#272523`.
- **Power-Up / Domain Buttons**: Mint (`#059669`) for reward claim buttons; Cyan (`#0284c7`) for academic study triggers.

### 2. Quest Cards (Task Tiles)
- **Container**: White surface with 48px rounded corners (`rounded-xl`), 24px internal padding, and a 4px resting extruded bottom edge (`#e8ded2`).
- **Domain Identity**: The card features an 8px thick vertical pill indicator along the left internal margin, colored according to the task domain (Orange for active chores, Cyan for school, Violet for creative play).
- **Header Structure**: Task name set in Outfit `title-md`, estimated completion pill badge (e.g., "⚡ 10 MIN"), and a dedicated mechanical completion target.

### 3. Checkboxes & Completion Hubs
- **Form**: 40px × 40px oversized rounded square (`rounded` with 16px radius).
- **Inactive State**: 3px solid border in `#ded2c3` on white.
- **Completed State**: Instant shift to Success Mint (`#10b981`) with a 3D pressed drop, accompanied by a bold white checkmark and a brief tactile scale bounce.

### 4. Category Chips & Streak Pills
- **Category Chips**: Full pill capsules, 36px tall, using warm pastel backgrounds (e.g., `#fff1e6` for orange tasks) with high-contrast text (`#c25200`) and a bold icon prefix.
- **Streak Pill**: Amber background (`#fef3c7`), displaying a dynamic flame icon with bold numerical count in `#b45309`.

### 5. Input Fields
- **Architecture**: 56px tall pill-shaped input (`rounded-full`) or 24px rounded rectangle. 2px solid boundary in `#ded2c3`, filled with `#ffffff`.
- **Text Entry**: Locked to Lexend `body-lg` (18px) to prevent accidental mobile browser zoom and maintain clear reading clarity.
- **Focus Ring**: Saturated 3px ring in Vibrant Orange (`#ff6b00`) with an ambient outer aura (`rgba(255, 107, 0, 0.2)`).

### 6. Gamified Progress Meters & Level Bar
- **Progress Track**: 24px tall chunky pill track with an extruded sunken container background in `#f2eae1`.
- **Fill Engine**: Gradient transitioning from `#ff6b00` into `#f59e0b` capped with an animated gloss streak, conveying tactile energy charging up toward completion.
